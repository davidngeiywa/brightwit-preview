About the college

A registered vocational training centre on Ngong Road

Brightwit College exists to make career-relevant, hands-on training accessible to every Kenyan. Our classes are small, our instructors are working professionals, and everything you learn is practised until you can do it for real: in a salon, behind the wheel or behind the bar.

We are registered by the Technical and Vocational Education and Training Authority (TVETA) as a Vocational Training Centre under the TVET Act, Cap. 210A.

Admissions

How to apply

Applying is simple. Complete four short steps and our admissions team will guide you through the rest.

1

Choose your programme

Review Beauty, Driving and Barista to find the one that matches your goals and schedule.

2

Submit your application

Apply online or visit the campus. We confirm receipt within one working day.

3

Interview and document check

A short conversation with an admissions officer to confirm your programme and verify your documents.

4

Enrol and start learning

Pick your morning, evening or weekend class and begin training.
