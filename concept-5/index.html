Why Brightwit

A college built around the job you want.

Brightwit College opened in 2018 at Kony House on Ngong Road. We teach three trades, and we teach them the way they are done at work: on real salon stations, in a training fleet, and on commercial espresso machines.

Classes run in the morning, evening and at the weekend. When you finish, you leave with a certificate from a TVETA-registered centre, an industry attachment behind you, and help finding a job or starting your own business. More than 2,500 people have done it since we opened, and 92% of them are placed in work.

How to apply

What you get as a student

Practical-first classes

Real salon stations, a training fleet and commercial espresso machines. You learn by doing the work.

Flexible schedules

Morning, evening and weekend classes, so you can train around a job or family.

Career support

Industry attachments, placement help, and guidance if you want to start your own business.

Certified and regulated

A registered vocational training centre under the TVET Act, Cap. 210A.
