Why students pick Brightwit

We opened in 2018 with one idea: teach the way the job is actually done. So you learn makeup on real clients, drive a real training fleet on real roads, and pull shots on the same espresso machines the city’s cafés use.

Classes are small enough that your trainer knows your name and where you are getting stuck. When you finish, our career team helps with attachments, job placement, and guidance if you would rather start something of your own.

We look after you from day one

Learning a trade is easier when the school fits around your life and stays with you after graduation.

  • Practical-first classes

    Real salon stations, a training fleet and commercial espresso machines. You spend most of your time doing, not watching.

  • Flexible schedules

    Morning, evening and weekend classes, so you can keep your job or look after family while you train.

  • Career support

    Industry attachments, placement help and guidance to start your own business when you are ready.

  • Certified and regulated

    Brightwit is TVETA-registered, so your certificate is recognised by employers across Kenya.
