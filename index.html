The layout you picked, repainted in Brightwit blue, navy and white, with the official crest and the new course list and fee structure.

Crimson Standard

Established, trustworthy, institutional. The college as a serious registered training centre.

Brand idea
Extends the Brightlink Recruitment diamond into a family identity: the same crimson and grey, a serif B inside a four-facet diamond, so the two companies look related.
Layout
Sharp corners, strong rules, a structured grid. Large photo frames, a dark student-support band, a calendar panel for the intake.
Motion
Headline rises word by word, the hero photo wipes in, numbers count up, programme cards unveil from the bottom, step numbers turn crimson as you pass them. Buttons fill with a sweep on hover.

Best if credibility with parents, employers and regulators matters most, and you want Brightwit and Brightlink to share a visual family.

Jua

Bright, energetic, youthful. Built for the 18 to 30 year old scrolling on a phone.

Brand idea
Jua is Swahili for sun. A sun-ray mark with a bold B, sun-yellow as the brand colour, and one signature colour per programme: rose for Beauty, sky blue for Driving, cocoa for Barista.
Layout
Bento tiles in the hero, a scrolling ticker, rounded cards with sticker fee badges, a horizontal photo strip, speech-bubble testimonials.
Motion
Tiles pop in with a spring, the ticker speeds up as you scroll, programme cards tilt in 3D under the mouse and their fee stickers wiggle, the sun badge rotates with scroll, buttons are magnetic.

Best if you want to stand out on social media and attract younger students who choose on feel.

Bloom

Soft, warm and lovely. Pastel grounds, rounded shapes, a friendly voice.

Brand idea
Four pastel petals (mint, peach, lilac) around a coral centre: the three programmes around one college. Each programme owns one pastel, and coral is reserved for buttons and links so the page stays calm.
Layout
A centred headline surrounded by floating photo cards, soft curved section edges, large pastel programme cards, a vertical admissions list with a line that draws itself.
Motion
The floating cards drift with the mouse and bob gently at rest, pastel shapes move slowly with scroll, the headline rises word by word on load, cards tilt softly under the mouse, the admissions line draws as you scroll.

Best if you want the warmest, most approachable feel, especially for the Beauty programme and for women applicants.

What is the same in all five

Your photos and your name

The campus and student photos you shared are in every concept, with the full name Brightwit College of Professional Studies. Extra imagery shows Kenyan and African subjects only.

TVETA registration, front and centre

Registered Vocational Training Centre, TVETA/PRIVATE/TVC/0138/2026, appears near the top, in the about section and in the footer of every concept.

Corrected intake calendar

The current site lists a January 2026 intake with a December 2026 deadline. All concepts show the next real intake: Open Day 15 Nov 2026, deadline 5 Dec 2026, classes from 12 Jan 2027.

Same content, same admin

Programmes, news and events, testimonials, gallery, messages and settings stay fully manageable from the admin, whichever design you pick.

Phone-first and accessible

Every layout is built for phones first. Menus collapse, grids stack, galleries scroll with a thumb, and all motion switches off for people who turn off animations on their device.

Mix and match

If you like the layout of one and the colours of another, say so. The identity and the layout can be combined.
