Why Brightwit

Small classes. Real tools. Trainers who do the job.

Brightwit College of Professional Studies was founded in 2018 to make career-relevant training accessible to every Kenyan. Everything you learn is practised until you can do it for real.

  • Practical-first classesReal salon stations, a training fleet and commercial espresso machines.
  • Flexible schedulesMorning, evening and weekend streams around work or family.
  • Career supportAttachments, placement help and guidance to start your own business.
  • Certified & regulatedRegistered under the TVET Act, Cap. 210A.
Admissions

Four steps and you're in.

Apply online or walk in to Kony House. We confirm every application within one working day.

1

Choose your programme

Beauty, Driving or Barista. Not sure? Talk to admissions and we'll help you decide.

2

Submit your application

Fill in the short form online or visit the campus. Receipt confirmed within a day.

3

Interview & document check

A quick chat with an admissions officer to confirm your programme and documents.

4

Enrol and start
