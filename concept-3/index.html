The college

Registered. Practical. Personal.

Brightwit exists to make career-relevant, hands-on training accessible to every Kenyan. Classes are small, instructors are working professionals, and everything is practised until you can do it for real.

We are registered by the Technical and Vocational Education and Training Authority as a Vocational Training Centre under the TVET Act, Cap. 210A, and our driving programme follows the NTSA curriculum.

How to apply

I

Choose your programme

Beauty, Driving or Barista. Our admissions team will help you match a programme to your goals and schedule.

II

Submit your application

Online or in person at Kony House. We confirm receipt within one working day.

III

Interview and document check

A short conversation with an admissions officer to confirm your programme and verify your documents.

IV

Enrol and begin

Choose your morning, evening or weekend class and start training in January.
