Language levels explained

What each German and French level means

Brightwit's language courses follow the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), the scale used by universities and employers abroad. Fees are charged per level, and you can start at the level that matches you.

A1Beginner

Introduce yourself, ask and answer simple questions, interact in a simple way and complete a transaction at a shop, restaurant or bank.

A2Elementary

Understand frequently used phrases and expressions, communicate about everyday matters and handle short social exchanges when travelling.

B1Intermediate

Get by in common situations, describe experiences, events and ambitions, and give reasons and explanations for opinions and plans.

B2Advanced

Understand the main ideas of complex texts, interact with native speakers with a strong degree of fluency and discuss abstract topics.

About the college

A TVETA and NITA accredited college in Ngong

Brightwit College of Professional Studies equips learners with practical skills, industry-relevant knowledge and professional competencies for employment, self-employment and career advancement.

The college emphasises hands-on learning to prepare students for the workplace and for entrepreneurship. Through technical, vocational and professional education, Brightwit gives learners the skills and the confidence to succeed in their chosen fields.

Our vision

To be a leading TVET institution in providing quality, practical and market-driven skills for employability and entrepreneurship.

Our mission

To equip learners with relevant technical, vocational and digital skills through quality training, practical learning and industry-oriented education.

How to join Brightwit

Four short steps. Apply online or walk into the campus at Kony House, and the admissions office guides you through the rest.

1

Choose your course

Compare the four schools and the fee structure above, or call the admissions office for advice on which course fits your goals.

2

Apply online or at the campus

Fill in the online application below. The admissions office confirms receipt within one working day.

3
